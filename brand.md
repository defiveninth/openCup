# orderCup

**orderCup** is the product name. Keep this capitalization in every language: lowercase `order`, uppercase `C`, lowercase `up`.

The brand mark is the existing three-stripe icon. Use it beside the orderCup wordmark in headers and login screens, and by itself for the app icon. Its SVG is `src/app/icon.svg`; the header version is the `.brand-mark` in `src/components/shell.tsx`.

Keep the black-and-white visual identity: near-black `#111111`, white `#FFFFFF`, and neutral grays for secondary text and surfaces. Use clean sans-serif typography and direct, helpful language.

The product connects restaurant menus, shared ordering, kitchen service, and management. Translate interface copy into Kazakh, Russian, and English; keep the orderCup name unchanged. The shared product name lives in `src/lib/brand.ts`.

**Table & Thyme** is the demo restaurant within orderCup. Restaurant names, menu content, QR destinations, and mock credentials belong to the tenant and are separate from the product brand.
